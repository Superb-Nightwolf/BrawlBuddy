Buffies are abilities introduced in the [[Version History#Update 65: Buffies!|December 2025 update]]. They enhance the abilities of a Brawler's Gadget, Star Power, and Hypercharge. Gadget Buffies and Star Power Buffies either add a second effect or buff the existing effect, while Hypercharge Buffies all make the Hypercharge last two more seconds and increase the effectiveness of the main attack in addition to the Super buff it has at base. Buffies can be unlocked by spending [[Coins]] and [[Power Points]], [[Gems]] or [[Vault Keys#Buffie Keys|Buffie Keys]], and can be obtained randomly from [[Chaos Drops|Ultra Chaos Drops]]. Buffies are available at 1000 Trophies, but if the player unlocks one by a special gacha or an Ultra Chaos Drop, the menu is instantly unlocked. Once a Buffie is unlocked, it can also closely follow a Brawler around as a cosmetic item. Each Buffie also unlocks a new color option, though as long as one is unlocked its effects will be permanently active. After collecting all the Buffies in a claw machine, the player can spend [[Bling]] to unlock a Bling Buffie, which adds a fourth color option to the Buffie. Bling Buffies are purely cosmetic and do not affect gameplay.

==Cost==
When choosing to buy the Buffies with resources, players cannot choose which Buffie they want to unlock. Instead, one will be selected at random by the claw machine. Bling Buffies can only be pulled out of the claw machine for 10000 [[Bling]] after unlocking all Gadget, Star, and Hyper Buffies from that claw machine. Alternatively, specific Buffies can be bought for Gems or a [[Buffie Key]]. Each claw machine contains one Trio of related Brawlers. With 3 Buffies for each Brawler, there are 9 total Buffies per claw machine.

==History==
*On 16/12/25, the combined Ranger Ranch and Mortis' Mortuary Claw Machine were added to the game.
*On 25/02/26, the combined Bull's Diner and Rumble Jungle Claw Machine were added to the game.
*On 22/04/26, Buffie Claw Machines now include only one Trio per machine instead of two, and the existing Claw Machines were split accordingly. Instead of getting a free Buffie per new Claw Machine, players get a random Buffie per update. The Gift Shop Claw Machine was added to the game.
*On 30/06/26, the Arcade and Super City Claw Machines were added to the game. The UI was changed so each Trio Claw Machine has a separate tab.
